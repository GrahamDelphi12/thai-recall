The problem

You understand more than you can say in time.

Most learners eventually understand far more Thai than they can actively recall. One missing phrase arrives at exactly the wrong moment — and the moment moves on.

Thai Recall helps you continue speaking. Look up what you need, hear it once, repeat it, and stay with the person in front of you.

Used language

This is not learned language. This is used language.

One earbud keeps you in the room. High-fidelity Thai plays while your other ear stays open to the café, the market, the person speaking.

Built for expats, partners, retirees, and long-term visitors who already have vocabulary — and need speed when life is loud.

Creator

Graham Osborn

“I built the tool I wished I had when face-to-face Thai moved faster than my grammar.”

Graham’s path into Thai didn’t start in a software lab — it started on the ground, living inside the culture. Like many long-term residents, he hit the wall: strong vocabulary, slow real-time production.

Drawing on postgraduate work in Educational Technology and ELT at The University of Manchester (MEd, 2003), he designed Thai Recall around situational phrases, learner autonomy, and the phonological loop — including the single-earbud technique that keeps one ear in the live conversation.
