Thai Recall

Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 1 August 2026

Thai Recall is designed to help language learners build and use their own personal translation library while respecting their privacy.

Information We Collect

Thai Recall does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location.

Translation Requests

When you request a translation that is not already stored in your local library, the text you enter is sent securely to the Google Cloud Translation API in order to obtain a translation.

These requests are processed by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy.

Local Storage

Your saved words, phrases, favourites and notes are stored only on your own device. Thai Recall does not upload your personal library to our servers.

Advertising

Thai Recall does not display advertisements.

Analytics

Thai Recall does not use analytics or tracking software.

Data Sharing

Thai Recall does not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

Children

Thai Recall is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
