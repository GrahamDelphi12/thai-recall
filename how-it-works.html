The science of speed

From missing word to spoken phrase

How Thai Recall bridges the gap between hearing what you need and saying it while the conversation is still alive.

1

Open your situational toolkit

Search English for the phrase you almost know, or browse curated packs — markets, medical, travel, idioms. Built on the lexical approach: ready-made chunks beat building sentences word by word under pressure.

2

Patch gaps in real time

If it isn’t in your library yet, look it up. Thai Recall fetches translation and pronunciation, then lets you save it to your personal list — learner autonomy, not a fixed syllabus.

3

Single-earbud acoustic anchor

Listen in one ear. Keep the other open to the room. You copy tone and rhythm while staying socially present — phonological loop plus output practice, without isolating yourself from the person you’re talking to.

4

Lock it with recall practice

Favourites and spaced repetition bring phrases back when memory needs work. Retrieval practice moves language from “I know it somewhere” to “I can say it now.”
