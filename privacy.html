Privacy

How Thai Recall handles lookups and the phrases you save.

Lookups

When you search for a phrase that is not already in your local library, the app may send that query to Google Cloud Translation to obtain a translation used for display and speech playback.

On-device storage

Saved phrases, favourites, and spaced-repetition state are stored on your device so you can recall them quickly offline.

What we don’t do

Thai Recall does not harvest, sell, or rent your personal search history or custom phrase library to third-party advertisers.
